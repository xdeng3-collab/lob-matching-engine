How to read this:
  * Quote the median, and read the range as the machine rather than the
    book. A single replay is not reproducible on a laptop: inside one
    9-repeat invocation the speedup ranged 3.4x to 20.7x. The median is:
    two such invocations gave 15.3x and 15.0x. That is why this prints
    both, and why a benchmark that reports one number from one run is
    reporting the scheduler.
  * Every tail sample carries the 42.0 ns now() round trip, and the clock
    advances in 41.0 ns steps. That is noise against a p99.9 in
    microseconds and a large fraction of a p50 in the tens of ns, which
    is why cost belongs in the amortised table and only the tail belongs
    in this one.
  * The map baseline only inserts and cancels -- it never matches -- so it
    is being flattered. The flat book is doing strictly more work.
